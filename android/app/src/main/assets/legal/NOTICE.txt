1. adreno-llms

   Source: https://github.com/a8nova/adreno-llms
   Source revision used: 2f1aec494edf51536d130c0ba99c60676b0ff6fb

   The Kokoro-82M native inference runtime, OpenCL kernels, model build logic,
   persistent streaming protocol, and Android native-process foundations are
   derived from adreno-llms. They were modified in 2026 to form a standalone
   Android system TTS engine and automatic Kokoro installer.

2. VoxSherpa TTS

   Source: https://github.com/CodeBySonu95/VoxSherpa-TTS
   Source revision consulted: cdb6843f4998f4bba2f7a46c1f4a897c269fa4aa
   Copyright (C) 2025 CodeBySonu95
   License: GNU General Public License version 3 or later

   VoxSherpa's Android system-wide TTS service, voice-discovery, settings, and
   synthesis-callback architecture were used as the reference and basis for
   this project's system-engine integration. VoxSherpa's neural inference
   engines and other application features are not included.

3. Kokoro

   Source: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro
   Source revision consulted: dfb907a02bba8152ca444717ca5d78747ccb4bec
   Model: https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
   License: Apache License 2.0

   The application automatically downloads converted Kokoro-82M weights and
   28 US and UK English style voices from a8nova/adreno-llms-weights. The
   downloaded model files are separate from the application source and retain
   their upstream Apache-2.0 terms.

4. eSpeak NG

   Source: https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng
   Version: 1.52.0 (revision 4870adfa25b1a32b4361592f1be8a40337c58d6c)
   License: GNU General Public License version 3 or later, with supporting
   files under the additional licenses included in LICENSES/.

   eSpeak NG is built into the Kokoro native executable to perform on-device
   grapheme-to-phoneme conversion. Its English language data downloads on the
   first application launch.
